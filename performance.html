How this was tested

Historical simulation in MetaTrader 5 on XAUUSD (gold), 1 January 2020 to 8 October 2026. Starting balance $100,000, leverage 1:100, all profits reinvested. Every trade has a stop loss and is sized from the account balance according to the risk profile.

Important

Past performance, real or simulated, does not guarantee future results. Trading leveraged gold contracts carries a high risk of loss, and losses can be larger than those shown. Only trade with money you can afford to lose.
