### 9 Nov 2026: Binance, eToro, Revolut, Bridgewater, Man Group
(binance.com and etoro.com were blocked by the session network; these findings come from help-centre articles, press releases and reviews.)

**They do better**
- Binance: one written PnL formula on every screen, and Proof of Reserves.
- eToro: a 1-10 risk score, a benchmark comparison and a "stats updated" stamp.
- Revolut: a documents hub (monthly statements), alerts at each 10% loss, and very clean cards.
- Bridgewater: principles and research build trust.
- Man Group: factsheets in a fixed format.

**FTM does better**
- Deposit and withdrawal markers on the balance chart.
- Calendar, statement and performance in one place.
- 50 languages on the web.
- Live public results.

**Built today**
- "How we count" tips on every dashboard figure.
- Show more / Show less lists.
- Profit by period card.
- Redesigned balance chart.

**Next, ranked**
1. Monthly statement PDF. BUILT 9 Nov (Accounts > Account activity > Download statement).
2. Freshness stamp ("updated Xs ago").
3. Risk gauge 1-10 next to the chosen profile (engine figure).
4. Factsheet strip: month, YTD, 12m, annualised, best and worst month.
5. "Verify my account" card with a history checksum (engine).

### 10 Nov 2026: Interactive Brokers, Coinbase
- Interactive Brokers (PortfolioAnalyst): better than FTM: compares the account with up to 3 benchmarks (S&P 500 total return, world indices), toggles time-weighted vs money-weighted return, GIPS-style method, downloadable reports. FTM better: one-tap, phone-first, live trades on the chart, plain language, every figure checked automatically.
  Ideas: (1) compare the balance % with a market on the same chart (BUILT 10 Nov: Gold / US Tech 100 / Bitcoin, real daily prices from FTM's feed); (2) a money-weighted return next to the time-weighted one, explained in one line; (3) a quarterly PDF report with benchmarks.
- Coinbase: better than FTM: one big balance with a time-frame graph, per-asset "Portfolio %", net invested vs return explained simply. FTM better: true time-weighted %, drawdown, risk level, trade-by-trade history, sessions and calendar.
  Ideas: (1) "net invested" (deposits - withdrawals) shown beside total return on the blue card; (2) per-market share of the balance (by market card already shows trade share).

### 11 Nov 2026: Robinhood, Charles Schwab
(robinhood.com and schwab.com do not resolve from the session network; findings come from their own help and guarantee pages found by search.)
- Robinhood: better than FTM: one very simple portfolio chart (today's return in money and %, quick time spans), annualized returns shown for periods of 1 year or more on managed "Strategies", comparison with a market benchmark, return method named (Modified Dietz). FTM better: true time-weighted chaining from every closed trade, drawdown and risk level from the engine, trade-by-trade history, calendar, sessions, per-period statistics pop-ups, any display currency, 49 languages.
  Ideas: (1) average yearly return for periods of a year or more, with the method explained in one line (BUILT 11 Nov: "Per year (average)" + "Time-weighted…" note in every period pop-up); (2) a one-tap "share my result" card for the Community (nickname + %, no amounts).
- Charles Schwab: better than FTM: a written Satisfaction Guarantee (refund of eligible fees if a client is not satisfied, limits stated plainly), corporate fact sheet with size and client numbers, daily monitoring and automatic rebalancing explained simply. FTM better: live, per-trade transparency; every figure straight from the broker statement; instant client area on phone.
  Ideas: (1) an "FTM promise" page (for Gospel to decide: e.g. subscription fee refund within N days if not satisfied; never refunds market losses), (2) a one-page FTM fact sheet (members, markets, live since, figures from the engine).
