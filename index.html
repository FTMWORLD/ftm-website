    How your profit share works

    • Lock period. You become eligible on the 1st of the month after 13 full months. Join in September 2026 and you are eligible on 1 November 2027.
    • Your share. Half of the percentage the account made between the day you joined and the day you become eligible, applied to the amount you invested.
    • Minimum. The smallest investment FTM accepts is 5.000€.
    • Example. The account makes 40% over your period. Your share is 20% of what you invested, so 2.000€ on 10.000€.
