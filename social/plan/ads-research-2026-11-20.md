# What the biggest fintech/trading brands do in short video ads (research 20 Nov 2026)
Source: secondary coverage and ad-policy pages (direct ad libraries were not reachable from the work machine).

## Common to the best short ads
- Brand + message in the first 3 seconds (TikTok: 63% of top-CTR ads; Meta/Toluna: brand+message in 5 s = 1.7x more likely top-20%).
- 9:16 full screen, 15-30 s cut-downs of a longer hero film.
- Native, creator-style, direct to camera beats polished; speech + music; product shown more than once (2.7x); one clear feature (5.3x).
- On-screen text in every frame for mute viewing; real app screen; one CTA; branded end card (Google/Kantar ABCD: up to +30% short-term sales likelihood).

## Regulated brands
- Almost never show returns. Robinhood: ambition/ownership. Public.com: real members. eToro: reach, AI-made visuals. Trading 212: education series. Plus500: "Trade with Clarity".
- UK ASA bans in 2026 for trading-tool/AI-trading video ads that implied profit with little risk; risk line must be in the ad itself.
- Meta: no "get rich quick"/unrealistic outcomes, licence may be required. TikTok: restricts forex/high-risk investment ads, no paid creator posts for trading platforms.

## Examples
eToro "eToro is Everywhere" (2025, made with Google Veo 3, 15 languages) · Coinbase QR-code Super Bowl ad · Public.com member stories · Wealthfront "My Recipe for Retiring at 40" · Monzo TikTok trend bursts · Revolut "Money Possibilities" (ownable wool world).

## Lessons for FTM (ranked)
1. Paid ads: stop opening with big % figures; hook = honest proof ("Results posted every night at 11 p.m. Watch them free").
2. Product as hero: real client-area screen recordings in a phone frame, the 11 p.m. result arriving like a notification; FTM + message in first 3 s; product twice.
3. 9:16, 15-30 s, one idea; captions every frame; speech + music; end card "Open a free account".
4. Stop cartoon characters, robotic TTS, generic synthetic music: real human voice (founder or member) and an ownable navy/gold look (AI-generated realistic visuals are fine, as eToro shows).
5. Edutainment series: "How FTM MACHINE works in 20 s", myths, "what happened tonight".
6. Member stories (with consent, no promises).
7. Paid ads need a readable risk line in the ad (conflicts with CLAUDE.md risk rule: owner decides for paid ads).
8. Test 3-5 hooks per idea, refresh every 2-3 weeks, boost organic winners; no paid creator posts for trading on TikTok.
