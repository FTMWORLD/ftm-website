How we get more people to see FTM

Two research rounds: how small finance brands grow on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and Facebook, and what makes paid finance video ads work. Below: where we stand, the ten actions that matter most, which ad to pay for, and three decisions only you can make.

We are far below normal. That points to the accounts not being shown to new people yet, not only to the videos.

The ten actions that matter most

  1. Check each account can be recommended YouInstagram: Settings → Account status → "Can be recommended". TikTok: Settings → Account status. If either is red, every other step is wasted until it is fixed.
  2. Win the first second We doFrame 1 shows the payoff in big words (the result, the question, the number). We now track "skip rate" and "viewed vs swiped away" every week and change the openers that lose people.
  3. Make videos people send to a friend We doInstagram's strongest signal for reaching new people is sends. A result slide is rarely sent; "What €5 a day becomes" or "Why your savings lose to inflation" is. Half of the morning videos become send-to-a-friend explainers.
  4. One recognisable series We doThe nightly post becomes a numbered series with a fixed opener: "Night 1 of FTM MACHINE, live". Finance creators who grew fast (Vivian Tu, Humphrey Yang) used one repeated opener and a daily streak.
  5. Turn numbers into things you can see We doCoins stacking, a jar filling, a calendar of green and red days. Humphrey Yang's rice grains for $1 billion drew 2.2 million views.
  6. Right length per job We doNightly result: 10–20 s, built to loop. Morning explainers: 30–60 s. Ads: 21–34 s (TikTok's own best range).
  7. Search words, not hashtag piles We doSay, show and caption the same plain phrase ("how to start investing with little money"). Instagram now allows 5 hashtags at most; we use 3–5 specific ones.
  8. Every video different and original We doPlatforms stop recommending repeated or look-alike videos. Posting our own clean file on all four platforms is fine; the risk is sameness, so the opener, look and script change every day.
  9. Comments that start conversations We doA pinned question under every post, video replies to good comments, and "comment GUIDE for our free starter guide" (a guide, never a sign-up or a figure).
  10. Collab posts with small creators You choose whoSmall brands get about 5× more interactions on accepted Instagram collab posts. Unpaid and educational only: paid creator promotion of trading is banned on TikTok and restricted in the UK and Nigeria.

Which ad to pay for

Couple ad (recommended)

  • The question hook feels native in the feed.
  • "Even the bad days?" admits losses: builds trust and passes ad review more easily.
  • "Pause it any time" shows the customer stays in control.

Fixes we are making: words on screen from frame 1 ("Real results, posted every night at 11"), a 20-second cut, the risk line large and on screen for 3 seconds, cleaner voices.

Your voice ad

  • The 23:00 clock hook is strong.
  • The phone recording costs trust: research shows poor sound makes a speaker seem less credible.
  • The photo people are AI-made, so Meta adds an "AI info" label.

Fixes we are making: words on screen from frame 1, cleaner sound, a 15-second cut. Best with a re-recording in a quiet room, phone close to the mouth.

First test: Meta only (Instagram and Facebook Reels and Stories), objective Leads, financial-products ad category, 2 ad sets at €10–20 a day for 7 days. Judge by how many stop scrolling, how long they watch, clicks and cost per free sign-up.

Three decisions only you can make

  • Big percentage headlines in paid ads. Meta rejects ads with result claims that look unrealistic, and UK and EU rules forbid them without warnings. Our rule "lead with +98,644%" will very likely get ads rejected. Recommendation: no big % in paid ads; show daily results honestly in organic posts.
  • Countries. Meta allows UK finance ads only from FCA-authorised firms. Nigeria's SEC now requires registration to promote investment services. Recommendation: first test outside the UK, EU and Nigeria, or get those approvals first.
  • Platforms for paid ads. TikTok treats forex and trading as restricted, so our paid ads there will probably be refused. YouTube needs Google's financial services verification first. Recommendation: start on Meta only.
