FTM intro video: brief and script

An animated story video (about 90 seconds) that introduces FTM on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube. It is the first post on every account.

Goal

  • Show the everyday problem of busy people: no time for the markets, worry about the future and retirement.
  • Show the traps: waking up at night to trade, copying other people's trades.
  • Connect all these lives, then introduce FTM MACHINE as the solution.
  • Show how to start: open ftmwealthnation.com, create a free account, connect the account in seconds, then follow every trade and get daily, weekly and monthly results.
  • End with a clear call to action and a little FOMO: "The markets moved today. Did your money?"

Audience

Everyone, not only people who trade: nurses, pilots, bankers, drivers, parents. Call them "people", never "traders".

Style

  • Animated characters (flat, modern, friendly), smooth movement, calm music, a warm male or female narrator.
  • FTM colours: navy #0B1440 / #152a7a, blue #1F3A93, gold #D4AF37 / #E3B62F, red #E5323E (buttons), white. Font Inter, bold headlines.
  • Logo: blue rounded square with a gold rising chart line, plus the white word FTM. Tagline: "Invest today, secure tomorrow."
  • Vertical 9:16 (1080×1920), subtitles on screen, mature and premium, never loud or cheap.

Rules (platform rules; the account can be closed otherwise)

  • No promises: never "guaranteed", "risk-free", "get rich", "passive income", "quit your job", no profit amounts or percentages.
  • No paid actors pretending to be customers, no fake testimonials.
  • If realistic AI people or voices are used, the post must be labelled as AI-made.
  • Do not name any broker.
