# FTM ad script: "Eleven o'clock" (two voices, about 35 seconds)

Story: a couple at home at 11 p.m. He smiles at his phone; she is curious and a little sceptical. Every question she asks is a question a new person has. Pictures: ChatGPT pack (couple, hand with phone, nurse, office, courier, kitchen, world) with real FTM screens in the phones.

| # | Who | Line | On screen |
|---|-----|------|-----------|
| 1 | HER | It's eleven o'clock. Why are you smiling at your phone? | Clock 22:59 -> 23:00, couple on the sofa |
| 2 | HIM | FTM just posted today's result. | Phone: real day result |
| 3 | HER | Every night? | |
| 4 | HIM | Every night. Every single trade. | Real trade list |
| 5 | HER | Even the bad days? | |
| 6 | HIM | Even the bad days. That's why I trust it. | Good day and bad day cards |
| 7 | HER | And who does the trading? | |
| 8 | HIM | FTM MACHINE. It trades on my own account, while I'm at work. | Nurse, office, driver, "Running" |
| 9 | HER | And if I want to stop? | |
| 10 | HIM | One tap. Pause it any time. | Pause switch |
| 11 | HER | So how do I start? | |
| 12 | HIM | Free account. Just your email. | Real sign-up screen |
| 13 | HER | (to camera) Link below. | End card |
| 14 | BOTH | See you at eleven. | "See you at 23:00" |

Recording: each person records their own lines as separate voice notes, in a quiet room (bedroom or car, no echo), phone 20 cm from the mouth, smiling, normal speed. Two or three takes of each line. Say "F-T-M" letter by letter and "FTM MACHINE" as "F-T-M Machine".
