# FTM – "Does FTM rest at the weekend?" – storyline and image prompts

Please create 9 realistic, high-quality images for a vertical video (Instagram Reel / TikTok / YouTube Short).

## Rules for every image
- Size: vertical 1080 x 1920 (9:16).
- Style: realistic photography with a warm, cinematic look. Natural light, shallow depth of field, real people, and a modern, premium feel (like an Apple or Revolut advert).
- People: a mix of ages and backgrounds (African, European, Asian, Latin American), happy and relaxed, aged 25 to 45.
- Colours: let FTM's colours show gently in the scene: navy blue (#0B1440), gold (#D4AF37) and a touch of red (#E5323E).
- **No text, no logos, no numbers and no phone screens with writing in the image.** We add all words and figures ourselves.
- Keep the **top 20%** and the **bottom 30%** of the image calm (sky, wall, floor or soft blur), because our titles and subtitles go there.
- Name the files weekend-01.jpg to weekend-09.jpg in the order below.
